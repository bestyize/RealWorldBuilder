# 草甸概念图提示词

## 草型
透明背景的写实植物标本式对照图，同一底线对比低矮狗牙根草毯、带毛刺穗的狗尾草和细羽状穗的芒草。无地面、花盆或参考平面。全株不裁切，柔和散射光，真实茎叶连接与叶片质感。

## scene-concept.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. A single landscape-format art-direction image for a visually elegant walkable 3D RealWorldBuilder meadow in late spring to early summer. THE GRASS IS THE SUBJECT, the tree is composition and flowers are a restrained accent. A quiet rolling temperate meadow with an unobtrusive very soft grassy foot track, layered depth, and one open-grown wide oak tree on the right third, broad canopy and believable limbs, tiny soft far hillside on the horizon. Camera is human-low at 0.9m above ground, slightly looking across the slope, natural 40mm lens, quiet clear late afternoon slightly hazy side light; pale ivory/sky and soft shadows, real documentary landscape with careful painterly composition, no cinematic teal/orange oversaturation, no bloom filter, no dewdrop macro. The low carpet of short Bermuda grass takes up approximately 75% of the ground and displays natural change in heights, mixed muted dark/sage grassy green and yellow-green edges. Irregular wisps of fine bending fescue roughly 2–4 times the ground layer edge height, scattered slender green foxtail seedheads on edges of taller tussocks; let the meadow breathe with substantial huge open expanses of grass. Small white English daisies with yellow center and white clover in soft, spatially separate tiny patches near the camera and along one side of the track. Very tiny blue speedwell flowers on the edge, rare low violet self-heal spikes; at ONE off-center group only put two soft vermilion field-poppy flowers, do not make a poppy field. Optional single anatomically realistic tiny cream yellow black Asian swallowtail in the foreground to supply a touch of motion, never a giant butterfly. Respect realistic relative scale: oak several meters away, small flowers only centimeters above grass. Distinct narrow leaves and no repeating scatter patterns. Avoid flower carpets, giant flowers, a formal garden, paving, rocks as main subjects, buildings, benches, people, mountains dominating, harsh HDR, fake CGI, text or borders. Wide 3:2 hero concept with foreground plant detail and uncluttered distance.

## low-plants-concept.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. One carefully spaced 3x2 wide botanical photo contact sheet, TRUE transparent alpha, showing SIX separate entire grassland specimens for 3D model reference. No text, panels, ground or soil platforms, no shadows outside the cutouts, no overlap, no objects cut by the borders, no visible checkerboard. Row one left: fine-leaved Festuca rubra red fescue natural airy separated tuft with many wire-fine slightly curved tapered soft sage green blades growing from the base, a very few slender ascending stems with spare fine seed sprays, no pampas plumes. Row one middle: white clover Trifolium repens, creeping stolon, several short stems holding individually small spherical white flower clusters and mostly trifoliate rounded leaflets with pale chevrons; one outer leaf on its own stem has exactly four round leaflets attached at one point. Row one right: Bellis perennis common daisy, low obovate green basal rosette and 2–3 short stems with petite white narrow-rayed flowers and small yellow disk, one in 3/4 side view. Row two left: Veronica persica Persian speedwell, with a short prostrate branching downy stem, small scalloped opposite leaves, several very small sky blue four petal flowers with white centers and thin darker stripes, fourth lower petal distinctly smaller. Row two middle: Prunella vulgaris self-heal, one small broad low growing non-woody clump, square stalk and opposite egg-shaped leaves with visible leaf joins, short stacked violet flowering heads, individual little purple hooded tubular flowers and green bracts, NOT lavender. Row two right: Papaver rhoeas red field poppy, exactly four paper-thin slightly wrinkled soft scarlet petals per flower, dark central stamens, slender softly hairy stalk and drooping unopened bud, pinnately cut leaves. Whole plants isolated, fine morphology for modeling, natural diffuse sunlight, matte cuticles and real petal translucency, realistic muted palettes, tack sharp focus. All six fill their own cells with blank gutters and safe padding. Subjects scaled to showcase individual anatomy, not a size comparison.

## oak-concept.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. One professional ultra detailed landscape-format contact sheet for game tree modeling design approval, truly transparent alpha. One MATURE OPEN-GROWN English oak, Quercus robur, main full-height landscape portrait on the LEFT occupying about 70 percent image width; at RIGHT with separate generous clear gap, one detached small stem bearing several individually clear smooth rounded-lobed simple oak leaves and two acorns on long stalks as a material reference. Main tree entire canopy and trunk fully in frame, no cropping. Short broad tapering deeply furrowed gray-brown trunk and visible basal flare no dirt mound; fork at relatively low trunk height, large sturdy mostly horizontal boughs with natural taper and asymmetries, smaller crooked forks, irregular broad crown composed of many discrete differently sized dense leaf clusters interspersed with small light gaps in which branching attaches visibly, not a uniformly solid sphere, no topiary, not a simple wallpaper tree. Fresh late-season natural olive green and muted small variations, no autumn orange, sunlight diffuse clear top-left with natural depth, crisp enough to model. Main tree near-orthographic side-three-quarter elevation at eye level with enough sky gap around branch silhouette, no fisheye. Isolated subjects only, actual transparent alpha, no written text, no backdrop, no cast shadow on invisible ground, no grass platform, no scenic mountains, no checkerboard. Make main silhouette strong and recognizable at thumbnail, detailed materials at zoom.

## swallowtail-concept.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Professional isolated macro photography design plate on genuinely transparent alpha, no labels, of ONE Asian swallowtail butterfly Papilio xuthus from TWO angles for 3D modeling approval. Place large quality dorsal wings spread open butterfly on left two thirds, a slightly smaller 3/4 side view of same specimen with partly raised wings on right, separated, both completely inside frame. Not monarch or swallowtail of North America. Cream yellow prominent dark black striped wing venation and bands typical of Papilio xuthus, tiny blue and orange hindwing marks, slender hindwing tails, two club-tipped antennae, slender segmented abdomen, clear thorax-to-wing connection and articulated legs in the side view, thickness of wing edge and scale texture subtly visible. No flower, no perch, no shadow outside cutout, no pins, no artificial glow, no human objects, no border, no names/watermark, no backdrop or visual checkerboard. Real macro focus, modest color, precise silhouette.
